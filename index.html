Our story

More than a
place to eat

Gusto was born from a simple idea — that great food and good company are the two ingredients no kitchen can manufacture. Nestled in the heart of the city, our lounge is designed to feel like a living room you never want to leave: warm amber lighting, plush seating, and a kitchen that takes every plate seriously.

Whether you're unwinding after work or celebrating something worth celebrating, Gusto meets you exactly where you are.
